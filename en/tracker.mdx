The tracker links advertising traffic with subscribers and conversions: where a click came from, who started the
bot and what happened next, all the way to a deposit. Reports, metrics and traffic-buying optimisation are built
on that data.

## What the tracker is for

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Counts the whole funnel" icon="filter">
    The platform records every click, bot start, subscribe, registration and deposit and links them to a specific
    subscriber by `click_id`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keeps the traffic source" icon="tags">
    For every subscriber you see which ad campaign, ad set and ad brought the click, down to the sub parameters.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Returns events to the ad account" icon="facebook">
    Registrations and deposits go back to Facebook through the Conversion API, and reach affiliates and CRMs as
    postbacks. Ad algorithms see real actions rather than clicks alone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optimises traffic buying for events" icon="bullseye">
    A campaign can be optimised not only for a click but for any funnel action: a subscribe, a dialog, a
    registration, a first deposit, a repeat deposit.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How the section is organised

In the company's left menu the tracker is a single "Tracker" item. Inside it, at the top, there is a horizontal
menu of eight tabs. It is not the left menu: the tabs switch the section's content while staying in place.

| Tab | What for | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Metrics, the funnel and campaign activity for a period. | below on this page |
| **Campaigns** | The campaign list, tracking links, statistics, editing. | [Campaigns](/en/campaigns) |
| **Landings** | Telegram clones and "Custom HTML" landings for FB traffic. | [Landings](/en/landings) |
| **Postbacks** | Connecting affiliate networks to receive postbacks. | [Postbacks](/en/postbacks) |
| **S2S Postbacks** | Sending conversion events to external services. | [S2S Postbacks](/en/s2s-postbacks) |
| **Reports** | Conversion and click logs with filters and CSV export. | [Reports](/en/reports) |
| **Domains** | Your domains for the tracking links of FB campaigns. | [Domains and proxies](/en/domains-proxies) |
| **Proxies** | Proxies for sending events to Facebook CAPI. | [Domains and proxies](/en/domains-proxies) |

<Info>
  The tracker sections are available to members with a tracker role ("Buyer", "Team lead buyer") and to company
  administrators. A member without a tracker role does not see the section at all. A buyer sees their own
  campaigns, a team lead buyer sees their buyers' campaigns and their own, and a company administrator sees
  everything. How to grant access is described in [Team and settings](/en/settings).
</Info>

## The click's path

The tracker collects the whole chain from an ad click to a conversion.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ad click" icon="arrow-pointer">
    A person follows a tracking link. A click is recorded: geo, device, browser, sub parameters, bot and proxy signals. The click is assigned a `click_id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Landing" icon="window-maximize">
    For an FB campaign your landing opens. The view is marked as an LP view. The button leads into the bot with a link like `t.me/<bot>?start=<click_id>`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bot start" icon="robot">
    The person presses the button and sends `/start`. A subscriber appears and enters the funnel, tied to the campaign and the `click_id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funnel and conversions" icon="filter">
    The subscriber walks the funnel: subscribe, first message, registration, deposits. Conversions from the affiliate network are tied to the subscriber by `click_id`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A landing click and a bot start are different events. A landing click is a hit on the promo page, and it may
  never reach the bot. Starting the bot with `/start` creates a subscriber and opens the funnel. In reports they
  are separated: clicks on the tracking link in one tab, bot starts in another.
</Note>

## Dashboard

The dashboard gathers the picture across all campaigns for the selected period. The period is switched with the
tabs "Today", "Yesterday", "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days", "All time" and "Period" ("Last 7 days" by default).
Next to them there is a filter by a specific campaign. A custom range holds no more than 365 days.

Each period is a calendar range of dates rather than the last N hours: "Today" shows today's date from midnight,
"Yesterday" the whole of yesterday, "Last 7 days" exactly seven dates including today. In a custom period both
chosen dates are included in the range.

<Note>
  Dates are counted in UTC. If you work in a zone east of UTC, events at night by your local time fall into the
  previous UTC date.
</Note>

The dashboard has four parts.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Metrics" icon="gauge">
    Tiles with the key numbers: subscribes, contacts, registrations, FTD, RD, CR, R2D. The set of tiles is configured with the metric picker. Clicking a tile opens the matching report filtered by campaign.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trends" icon="chart-line">
    A chart of the chosen metrics over time. A metric tile can be put on the chart with a colour indicator or hidden from it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Activity" icon="bolt">
    A live feed with two tabs: "Clicks" (landing clicks) and "Bot starts" (direct `/start`). A row leads to the "Clicks" log filtered by that `click_id`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent conversions" icon="receipt">
    Fresh FTD, RD and registrations as soon as you open the tracker, without switching tabs. The FTD, RD and Reg chips filter the types you need.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  The "Recent conversions" card shows fresh conversions right at opening: in the morning you see FTD, RD and
  registrations without opening any report. A conversion row leads to the campaign page, and the subscriber chip
  opens the conversation with them.
</Tip>

## Funnel metrics

The metrics show the traffic funnel from click to deposit. Metric names are the same in every interface language
and are written exactly as they are.

The funnel stages go in this order: clicks, unique clicks, bot subscribes, contacts (a first message from the
subscriber), registrations, FTD, RD.

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Click2LP** | The share of clicks that reached a landing view. |
| **LP2Bot** | The share of landing views that moved into the bot. |
| **Click2Bot** | End-to-end conversion from click to bot start. |
| **Click2Sub** | Clicks into bot subscribes. |
| **Sub2Con** | Subscribes into contacts (subscribers who replied). |
| **Click2FTD** | Clicks into first deposits. In the campaign list and on the dashboard tiles it is labelled CR. |
| **Reg2Dep** | Registrations into deposits. On the dashboard tiles it is labelled R2D. |
| **EPC** | Deposits amount per click (earnings per click). |

<Info>
  Besides the ready metrics, a company can create its own derived metrics from a formula over the base numbers
  (clicks, unique clicks, landing views, subscribes, contacts, registrations, FTD, RD, deposit amounts and spend) in
  [Tracker settings](/en/tracker-settings). They appear in the dashboard's metric picker automatically.
</Info>
