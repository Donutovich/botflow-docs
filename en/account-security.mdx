Your personal account settings, the "Security" group. Here you change your password, see which devices are
signed in, and find the "2FA" item.

## Change password

The "Change password" screen asks for your current password and the new one twice.

| Field | What you enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Current password** | The password you sign in with now. |
| **New password** | At least 8 characters. Don't reuse a password from a known breach. |
| **Repeat new password** | The same value, to rule out a typo. |

The "Save password" button applies the change. After a password change your other sessions are ended: you
will need to sign in again on other devices.

The platform will not accept a new password that matches the current one or that has appeared in known data
breaches. Choose another one in that case.

<Tip>
  A forgotten password is recovered from the sign-in page through the "Forgot password?" link: a reset link
  is sent to your email.
</Tip>

## Active sessions

The "Active sessions" screen shows the devices signed in to your account. The current device carries a "This
device" badge, and for the others you can see when the session was created and when it was last active.

- The **"Revoke"** button in a row closes a single session.
- The **"Revoke all other sessions"** button leaves only the current device.

<Warning>
  If the list has a device you don't recognise, revoke its session and change your password right away:
  revoking a session alone is not enough while the password stays the same.
</Warning>

## 2FA

The third item of the group, marked as coming soon. It holds no settings yet: signing in is confirmed
by your password, and the list of devices is in "Active sessions" above.
