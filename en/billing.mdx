A company's balance is topped up in USDT over the TRON network (TRC-20). Spending on tokens (AI operator replies
and message translation) and on running bots on the platform is charged from the balance. The section lives in
"Settings" → "Payments" → "Billing". The balance is held in dollars and topped up in crypto at the current rate.

<Note>
  The company owner or an administrator can top up the balance, change daily limits and request a refund. For
  operators and members without administrator rights the section is hidden.
</Note>

## Topping up the balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open «Billing» and press «Top up»" icon="wallet">
    "Settings" → "Payments" → "Billing". The "Top up" button opens the top-up dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount" icon="coins">
    The "Amount (USD)" field. The minimum top-up is $50. Press "Create invoice" and a payment page with the invoice opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the network" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Before showing the address the page asks you to confirm that you are sending USDT over the TRON (TRC-20) network. Press the confirmation button to reveal the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send USDT to the address" icon="qrcode">
    The page shows a QR code, the payment address, the amount in USDT and the network. Transfer exactly the stated amount to that address from your wallet or from an exchange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the credit" icon="circle-check">
    The status on the page updates automatically. Once the required number of network confirmations is reached the balance is topped up and the page can be closed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Send only USDT over the TRON (TRC-20) network. A transfer over another network (ERC-20, BEP-20, Solana) to this
  address means the funds are lost for good. Copy the address and the amount with the button on the page rather than
  typing them by hand.
</Warning>

### Invoice statuses

The payment page shows the current status and a countdown to the invoice's expiry.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting payment | The invoice is created, the transfer has not arrived yet. |
| Transaction detected | The payment is visible in the network and confirmations are being collected. |
| Confirming | The network confirmation counter is growing. |
| Confirmed | The funds are credited to the balance. |
| Received less than the amount | Less than expected arrived. The balance is topped up by the amount actually received; create a new invoice for the remainder. |
| Expired | The invoice's time ran out. |

<Info>
  If you sent the transfer and the invoice expired in the meantime, the funds are still credited; if that did not
  happen, write to support. A company can have up to 5 unpaid invoices at a time, so don't create a new one while
  an old one is still awaiting payment.
</Info>

## Promo codes

Next to the "Top up" button there is a "Promo code" field. Enter the code, press "Apply", and the code's amount is
credited to the balance. After applying you get a confirmation message, and a "Promo credit" row appears in the
transaction history.

## What the balance is spent on

Two categories of spending are charged from the balance:

- **Tokens.** AI operator replies to subscribers and message translation are charged: every AI reply and every
  translation reduces the balance. More about the AI itself in [AI operator](/en/ai-operator).
- **Running bots on the platform.** By the formula on the balance card: `$0.50 / bot / day + $0.05 / 1k messages`.
  The bot fee is charged per active bot per day, and messages are counted both ways: incoming from subscribers and
  outgoing from bots.

The "Current balance" card shows the amount in dollars and the charging formula.

### Token spending

The 30-day spending card shows the charges for tokens: what was charged over 30 days, the number of AI replies, the
token count and a breakdown by bot (the "Charged" and "Replies" columns). The "Accrued (not yet charged)" row shows
the current period's spending, which will be part of the next charge.

## Daily AI spending limit

The daily spending limit caps how much can be spent on AI replies per day. It is set at two levels.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="For the whole company" icon="building">
    "Settings" → "Company", the "Daily AI budget (whole company)" field. It caps the total charges for AI replies across every bot per day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For a single bot" icon="robot">
    The "AI Settings" page of the bot you need, the "Daily AI budget (this bot)" field. It caps the charges for that bot's replies only.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Under each field you see what has been spent today and how much is left until the limit. Once the daily limit is
reached the AI operator stops answering until midnight UTC and continues the next day. An empty field means no
limit. If both limits are set, the AI stops on whichever is reached first: the bot hitting its own budget or the
company hitting the shared one.

<Info>
  The limit covers AI operator replies only. The other funnel blocks (messages, buttons, delays) keep working even
  while the AI is paused. Budgets are changed by the company owner or an administrator, and the shared budget sits
  in [Settings](/en/settings).
</Info>

## Transaction history

The "Transaction history" card shows the last 50 balance operations. The columns are "Time", "Type", "Description",
"Amount USD" and "Balance after". Top-ups and credits are marked with a plus, charges go in minus, so the "Balance
after" column is convenient for checking how the balance changed.

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Top-up | A credit after an invoice was paid. |
| Promo credit | A credit from a promo code. |
| Charge | Payment for tokens (AI replies and translation) and for running bots. |
| Refund | Funds returned to the balance. |
| Adjustment | A balance change following your request to support. |
| Annual prepayment bonus | A credit for paying a year upfront. |
| Affiliate payout, Affiliate bonus | Credits from the referral programme. |

## Refunding the balance

To get an unused balance back, write to support and state the amount and the USDT-TRC20 address for the transfer.
The request is reviewed before the funds are sent.

## Referral programme

Invite clients through your link and earn a commission from their spending on the platform. The referral area opens
from the profile menu: your link, statistics on the people you invited and withdrawal of the accumulated commission
in USDT. More in [Referral programme](/en/referral).
