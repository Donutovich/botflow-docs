Invite clients through your links and earn a commission from their spending. The "Referral program" section opens
from the profile menu, is available to any account and consists of the **Home**, **Referrals** and **Payouts**
pages in the side menu.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a link and share it" icon="link">
    Every account has a main link, and you can create more, one per channel. The link leads to sign-up, and the companies invited through it appear in your referral list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your referral uses the platform" icon="users">
    When an invited company spends its balance on running bots and on messages, you are credited a commission from that spending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw what you earned" icon="wallet">
    The commission accumulates on the program's balance. Once the amount reaches the minimum, request a payout in USDT-TRC20.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The "Home" page

At the top of the page come the cards: the commission rate and all-time metrics. Below is the "Earnings, last 30
days" chart, which appears once there is at least one accrual in that period.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Commission rate" icon="percent">
    Your share of the invited companies' spending.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Referrals" icon="user-group">
    How many companies signed up through your links.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payable" icon="coins">
    The accumulated commission available for withdrawal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pending" icon="clock">
    The amount of withdrawal requests not yet completed: waiting for review, sending or network confirmations.
    The card shows while there are such requests.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid out" icon="circle-check">
    How much has already been paid to you, all time.
  </Card>
</Columns>

The spending of invited companies is shown per referral on the "Referrals" page.

Below the overview comes the "Links" block with a table of your links. For each link you see: the name, the link code
with a copy button, the offer and the landing, plus clicks, sign-ups, conversions and commission for the period
selected at the top of the page. Conversions are how many people invited through the link topped up their balance.
The main link cannot be deleted.

### Creating a link

The "Create link" button opens the form:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name" icon="pen">
    A name for the channel that makes sense to you, for example "Telegram channel". Only you see it, and it helps tell links apart in the statistics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Offer" icon="gift">
    An offer is the proposition for the invitee. The platform's main offer applies by default. The offer is **fixed to the link and does not change after creation**: to use another offer, create a new link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Landing" icon="browser">
    The page the invitee lands on. The home page by default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A ready link can be copied with the copy button in the row or from the "⋯" menu. The same menu has "Edit" (name and
landing) and "Archive". The main link cannot be archived.

## Commission

The default commission rate is **10%**; your rate is shown on the "Commission rate" card.

The commission is calculated from your referrals' **spending**: the fees for running bots and for messages. Spending
on AI operator replies is **not** part of the commission base. The accumulated amount is shown on the "Payable"
card, and the breakdown per invitee is on the "Referrals" page.

## The "Referrals" page

An anonymised breakdown by invitee: a "Referral #" label, the sign-up date and the amounts. For each referral you
see:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Referral | The invitee's anonymised label. |
| Date | When they signed up. |
| Stage | Signup, Active or Conversion. |
| Source | The name of the link they came through. |
| Offer | Which offer was applied. |
| Usage | How much the invitee spent. |
| Your commission | How much was credited to you from them. |

## The "Payouts" page

Payouts go in USDT over the TRON network (TRC-20), straight to the blockchain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the wallet address" icon="wallet">
    The "Wallet Address" field. Check the address: the transfer goes to the network and cannot be reversed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount" icon="coins">
    The "Withdrawal Amount" field. Above the form you see the available balance, and the "All" button fills in the whole available amount. There is a minimum withdrawal amount, stated in the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the payout" icon="paper-plane">
    Press "Withdraw". The request goes through review, and after approval the funds go to the network.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Withdrawal requests are collected in a table with the date, amount, status and a link to the transaction. While the
review and the transfer are in progress the status updates by itself. Below there is a "History": every operation of
the program, commission accruals with a plus and payouts with a minus.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | The request is created and waiting for review. |
| Approved | The request passed review. |
| Processing | The payout is being prepared for sending. |
| Sending | The transfer is going out to the network. |
| Sent | The transfer went to the network and confirmations are being collected (a confirmation counter is shown). |
| Paid | The funds are credited, and a transaction link is available. |
| Rejected | The request was rejected, with the reason given. |
| Failed | Sending did not succeed, with the reason given. |

<Info>
  If you already have a request in progress, its amount is shown in a separate note under the form, so you can see
  how much is already on its way. You can create a new request once the previous one is sent, rejected or failed.
</Info>
