The two logs answer different questions. The "Event log" shows what happened to your bots, while the
"Audit log" in the company settings shows who changed the company itself.

## Event log

The "Logs" section in the menu. A feed of bot events with filters by bot and by event type, plus a search in
the description. The list is paged with "Previous" and "Next", and today's events are marked with the time.

| Event | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Broadcast started** | You started a broadcast. |
| **Broadcast completed** | Sending finished. |
| **Broadcast cancelled** | A broadcast was stopped before the end. |
| **Flow created** | A new flow appeared in the builder. |
| **Flow updated** | The flow's schema was changed. |
| **Bot connected** | A bot was added to a workspace. |
| **Bot disconnected** | A bot was switched off or disconnected from the platform. |
| **Subscriber blocked** | A subscriber was blocked from their card or from the conversation. |
| **Subscriber deleted** | A subscriber was deleted. |
| **Subscribers cleaned up** | A bulk subscriber deletion took place. |

<Tip>
  When a funnel behaved differently from what you expected, start here: the event times show which flow edit
  or which broadcast caused it.
</Tip>

## Company audit log

Company settings, the "Team" group, item "Audit log". It collects changes to the company itself: creating and
deleting workspaces, role changes, blocks, ownership transfer.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Time** | When it happened. |
| **Actor** | Who did it. Platform actions are marked as "System". |
| **Action** | The type of change. |
| **Description** | Details of the change. |
| **Context** | What the change relates to. |

The "All types" filter narrows the list down to a particular action type.

<Info>
  The audit log is visible to the company owner and administrators. The bot event log is available to members
  with access to those bots.
</Info>
