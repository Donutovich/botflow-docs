Reports are two logs inside the tracker: "Conversions" and "Clicks". They show traffic events row by row: which
click arrived, who started the bot, which postback an affiliate sent and what the platform did with it. This is
where you check disputed cases and export data rather than look at totals (for totals there is the
[tracker dashboard](/en/tracker)).

## Where to find it

In the company's left menu open "Tracker", then the "Reports" tab. Inside the section there are two sub-tabs at
the top:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Conversions" icon="receipt">
    The log of affiliate postbacks and outgoing events to Facebook CAPI. Opens by default.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clicks" icon="arrow-pointer">
    The log of every click on your tracking links with geo, device and sub parameters.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
  Reports are visible to members with a tracker role ("Buyer", "Team lead buyer") and to company administrators
  with the "Tracker" capability. A member without a tracker role does not see the section. On granting access see
  [Settings](/en/settings).
</Info>

## Tools shared by both logs

Both logs are built the same way and share one set of tools at the top.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Period** | "Today", "Yesterday", "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days", "All time", "Period". Each period is a calendar range of dates: "Today" shows today's date, "Last 7 days" exactly seven dates including today. By default the log opens on today. |
| **Search** | A quick search by string (in conversions by `click_id`, in clicks by `click_id` or sub parameters). |
| **Filter** | A condition builder from fields and operators (in detail below). |
| **Columns** | The button shows and hides table columns. The choice is remembered for this company. |
| **CSV export** | Exports the current selection into a CSV file. |
| **Load more** | The log loads in pages of 50 rows, and the button loads the next one. |

<Tip>
  A row in either log can be expanded by clicking it: a card with every field of the event opens. In conversions
  those are all the parameters of the postback as the affiliate sent them, the request body sent to Facebook and
  the response; in clicks it is the full click card by groups (geo, device, sub parameters, FB, subscriber, flags).
</Tip>

## Conversions

The log brings two streams of events into one feed:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Incoming" icon="arrow-down-left">
    Postbacks from affiliate networks: registration, FTD, RD. The platform matches them to a subscriber by `click_id`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Outgoing" icon="arrow-up-right">
    Events the platform sends back to Facebook through the Conversion API (CAPI) for FB campaigns.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
  S2S postback sends are not in this log: their log lives in [S2S Postbacks](/en/s2s-postbacks) on the "Log" tab.
</Info>

The direction is switched with the "All / Incoming / Outgoing" filter. In the table incoming rows are marked with
an inward arrow and outgoing ones with an outward arrow.

### Columns

By default you see: time, direction, `click_id`, processing outcome, source, campaign. Through the "Columns"
button you can add "Type" (the event status) and "Error" (the error text, if the event did not go through).

### The outcome of an incoming postback

The "Outcome" column shows what the platform did with a postback. It is the main answer to "the affiliate says it
sent an FTD at 17:42 and I don't see it".

| Outcome | What it means |
|---|---|
| `success` | The postback was accepted and attached to a subscriber. |
| `no_click_match` | The `click_id` from the postback was not found: the click never reached the platform, or the id is wrong. |
| `duplicate` | Such an event already arrived earlier, the repeat was rejected. |
| `invalid_status` | The affiliate sent a status the platform does not accept. |
| `invalid_token` | The postback arrived with the wrong affiliate key. |
| `rejected_transition` | An invalid order of events for this click (for example, a repeat deposit before the first one). |
| `rate_limited` | The affiliate sent events too often and some were not accepted. Check the sending rate on its side. |
| `error` | The event could not be processed. The error text is visible in the "Error" column; if it repeats, write to support. |

### Outgoing to Facebook CAPI

For rows in the "Outgoing" direction the same column shows the delivery status to Facebook: "Sending" (in
progress), "Delivered" (Facebook accepted it), "Failed" (could not be sent). The same delivery is visible on the
campaign page in the "Facebook CAPI delivery" block. For which events go to CAPI and how to set that up see
[Tracker: overview](/en/tracker) and [Postbacks](/en/postbacks).

<Note>
  The search in conversions is by an exact `click_id`. Enter the `click_id` of a disputed conversion and one query
  shows its whole trail: whether the postback arrived, with which outcome, whether the event went to Facebook. If
  the log is open on a narrow period and the row is missing, switch the period to "All time": the postback may have
  arrived earlier.
</Note>

## Clicks

The log of every click on the company's tracking links. Each click is assigned a `click_id`, which the bot start,
the subscriber and the conversions are later tied to.

### Columns

There are many columns, and the set is configured with the "Columns" button. They cover almost the whole click
card:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Identity" icon="fingerprint">
    Time, `click_id`, IP, Referer, User-Agent, campaign, bot, source.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Geo" icon="location-dot">
    Country, city, region, language, ASN and AS Organization (the network provider).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Device" icon="mobile">
    Device type, model, OS and version, browser and version.
  </Card>
  <Card title="FB breakdown" icon="facebook">
    `fb_campaign`, `fb_adset`, `fb_ad`, placement. The values come from Facebook macros.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sub parameters" icon="tags">
    `sub1` … `sub5` from your tracking link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscriber and flags" icon="user-check">
    The subscriber (if the click reached the bot), "Unique", bot and proxy signals, "LP view".
  </Card>
</Columns>

The chip in the "Subscriber" column leads to the conversation with that person (see [Messages](/en/messenger)).
The "Bot" and "Proxy" signals mark suspicious traffic, "Unique" marks the first click from that device, and "LP
view" means the click actually reached a landing view.

### Click type: landing, /start, organic

A landing click and a bot start are different events, and in the log they are told apart by the "Type" column:

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Landing** | A visit through the tracking link, usually via a landing with a pixel. |
| **TG /start** | A direct bot start through a `t.me` link with the campaign code, without a landing. This is already an entry into the funnel. |
| **Organic** | A bot start without a campaign: the person found the bot themselves and sent `/start`. It lands in the "Organic" campaign. |

<Note>
  A landing click is a hit on the promo page, and it may never reach the bot. Starting the bot with `/start` creates
  a subscriber and opens the funnel. A live feed of events with separate "Clicks" and "Bot starts" tabs is in the
  "Activity" block on the [tracker dashboard](/en/tracker); clicking a row leads to the "Clicks" log filtered by
  that `click_id`.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The search in clicks looks by `click_id` or by any of the sub parameters. When you type a query the period
  switches to "All time" automatically, so the click you need is not cut off by the chosen dates. Clear the query
  and the period returns to today.
</Tip>

## The filter builder

The "Filter" button opens the condition builder. You assemble a rule from three parts: field, operator and value;
conditions inside a group are joined by "AND" or "OR", and groups between themselves by "OR". That is how you find,
for example, every click from a particular ad set over the last day that has no subscriber.

Fields are grouped by meaning: "Identity", "Geo", "Device", "Flags", "FB", "Sub tags", "Subscriber", and in the
conversion log also "Outcome". In the conversion log you filter by `click_id`, campaign, source, processing
outcome, status and error text.

There are sixteen operators, and the available set depends on the field type:

| Group | Operators |
|---|---|
| **Text** | equals, not equal, contains, does not contain, starts with, ends with, matches regex, does not match regex |
| **Lists** | has any of, has none of |
| **Numbers** | greater than, less than, greater or equal, less or equal |
| **Emptiness** | is empty, is filled |

## CSV export

The "CSV export" button exports the current selection honouring every filter, the period and the search. An export
returns up to 200 rows at a time, so for large slices narrow the selection with a filter or a period first. The file
opens in Excel or Google Sheets, or is uploaded into your own tracker.

<Warning>
  `click_id` is the money identifier for attribution: it ties a future deposit to a specific subscriber and
  campaign. In reports you can only view it and search by it; substituting it by hand is not allowed, otherwise the
  money attaches to the wrong person. More in [Postbacks](/en/postbacks).
</Warning>

## Visibility by role

The logs show only the data you have access to.

| Role | What is visible in reports |
|---|---|
| **Buyer** | Clicks and conversions of their own campaigns only. Postback parameters are shown in a shortened form. |
| **Team lead buyer** | The campaigns of all their buyers and their own. |
| **Company administrator** | Every click and conversion of the company, with all event fields in full. |

<Info>
  A tracker role is granted separately from a team role. How to set it up is described in
  [Settings](/en/settings).
</Info>
