BotFlow brings work with Telegram subscribers into one place: a funnel builder, conversations through bots, broadcasts, an AI operator and a conversion tracker. You don't need separate services for chat, broadcasts and analytics.

This page shows how the platform is organised and where to go for each feature. If you are just starting, open [Getting started](/en/getting-started).

<Info>
  An account has three levels: **company** (the top level) → **workspace** (a space for a group of bots and
  a team) → **bots** inside the workspace. The company is created automatically when you sign up, while the
  workspace and the bots you add yourself.
</Info>

## Three levels of the platform

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Company" icon="building">
    The top level of the account: team and roles, domains, the traffic tracker and billing. Everything shared across the project.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workspace" icon="folder">
    A space for a group of bots and a team. Each one has its own bots, broadcasts, campaigns and access
    grants, so different projects never overlap.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bot" icon="robot">
    Connected inside a workspace. A bot's sections are grouped by the work you do: conversations, audience, setup.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  You switch levels in the navigation bar at the top: Company › Workspace › Bot. The sections of the current
  level are always on the left.
</Tip>

## Company level

The top level of the account. Everything shared across the project lives here: incoming traffic, the team, domains and payment.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tracker" icon="chart-line" href="/en/tracker">
    Clicks, conversions and postbacks in one place. Optimises traffic buying in FB and Telegram Ads for
    events: registrations, deposits and redeposits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reports" icon="chart-simple" href="/en/reports">
    Live statistics on funnels, campaigns and subscribers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team and settings" icon="users" href="/en/settings">
    Members, roles and access to bots and the tracker.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domains and proxies" icon="globe" href="/en/domains-proxies">
    Your own domains for links and proxies for the tracker.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Billing" icon="wallet" href="/en/billing">
    Balance, top-ups in USDT-TRC20 and spending on tokens and bot operation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Postbacks" icon="arrows-rotate" href="/en/postbacks">
    Receiving events from affiliate networks: registration, deposit, redeposit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="S2S Postbacks" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="/en/s2s-postbacks">
    Sending conversion events to external services.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Workspace level

A space for a group of bots and a team. It holds the list of bots and everything you launch for the whole group at once: broadcasts, campaigns and landings.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Broadcasts" icon="paper-plane" href="/en/broadcasts">
    Mass broadcasts with media, buttons and scheduling. Automatic messages triggered by an event are set up
    in the flow builder.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Campaigns" icon="bullhorn" href="/en/campaigns">
    Traffic campaigns: source, domain, landing and buyer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Landings" icon="window-maximize" href="/en/landings">
    Generated and uploaded landings with a link into the bot.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) opens the command palette: search it to find bots, sections and actions without
  walking the menu. The same palette opens from the "Command…" button in the header.
</Tip>

## Bot level

A bot's sections are grouped by an operator's work: conversations, audience and setup.

### Conversations

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Messenger" icon="comments" href="/en/messenger">
    A single list of chats with subscribers: operators reply by hand or hand the conversation to the AI
    operator, apply tags and filter dialogs. In the bot menu this section is called "Messages".
  </Card>
</Columns>

### Audience

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Subscribers" icon="address-book" href="/en/subscribers">
    The full table of subscribers with their data, tags, funnel stage and tracking. Filters and bulk actions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tags" icon="tag" href="/en/tags">
    Labels for splitting subscribers. Tags are handy for filtering and for launching broadcasts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Filters" icon="filter" href="/en/filters">
    Filters are not a separate menu item: they live inside "Messages", "Subscribers", broadcasts and the
    "Condition" block. Chat list modes, quick funnel filters and your own condition sets.
  </Card>
</Columns>

### Setup

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Flow builder" icon="diagram-project" href="/en/constructor">
    A visual funnel builder: message and condition blocks joined by transitions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Settings" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/en/ai-operator">
    Persona, knowledge base, first message and reminders, model and budget of the AI operator.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Variables" icon="brackets-curly" href="/en/variables">
    Values like `{{first_name}}` for substitution into messages and conditions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bot settings" icon="gear" href="/en/bot-settings">
    Bot profile and avatar, activity, token, limits, moving between workspaces and API keys.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flow builder API" icon="code" href="/en/api-konstruktora">
    Starting a funnel and sending a callback from your code with the bot's API key.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  Not sure where to start? Walk through [Getting started](/en/getting-started): connecting the first bot and

## Documentation for AI

You can hand the documentation to a language model. The button at the top of every page copies it as Markdown
or opens it in ChatGPT and Claude, and appending `.md` to any page returns its text, for example
`docs.botflow.cc/en/constructor.md`.

<Tip>
  Hand those pages to your AI assistant so it builds funnels and answers based on the real product. The
  single-file version of the guide, `docs.botflow.cc/llms-full.txt`, carries the Russian text.
</Tip>
