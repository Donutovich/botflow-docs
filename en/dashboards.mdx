The Dashboard exists at three levels, and at each one it answers a different question: how the whole company
is doing, how a particular team is doing and how a particular bot is doing.

After you log in, the company owner and administrators see the company Dashboard. While the company has no
workspace yet, the "Get BotFlow running in 4 steps" checklist there starts with creating one. Everyone else
lands where they work: a manager or controller on the workspace Dashboard, an operator or anonymous operator in the bot's
"Messages", a buyer or team lead buyer in the "Tracker".

## Company dashboard

A summary across all of the company's bots: the subscriber base, messages, broadcasts and the state of the
bots. The funnel from the click to the deposit is not here, it lives in the [tracker](/en/tracker).

The period is switched with the tabs "Today", "Yesterday", "7 days", "30 days", "All time" and "Period". In
"Period" you set your own dates, up to 365 days. "7 days" opens by default.

Until every step is done, a "Get BotFlow running in 4 steps" checklist shows under the header: create a
workspace, connect a Telegram bot, create your first scenario and launch your first broadcast. The "Hide"
button removes it. Below is a line with the number of workspaces, bots and active subscribers.

### Metrics

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Total subscribers** | Your entire subscriber base across all of the company's bots, including those who have blocked the bot. |
| **Active subscribers** | Subscribers who haven't blocked the bot: you can deliver messages and broadcasts to them, except those the bot can't message first. |
| **New subscribers** | How many new subscribers arrived during the selected period. |
| **Net growth** | New subscribers minus those who unsubscribed or blocked the bot. Under the number you see how many came and how many left. |
| **Messages** | Incoming from subscribers and outgoing from bots, including broadcasts. |
| **Broadcasts** | How many broadcasts were sent during the period. "Delivered" under the number is the share of messages successfully delivered. |

The description of a metric opens from the icon next to its name. "New subscribers", "Messages" and
"Broadcasts" show next to the number by how many percent it changed against the previous period of the same
length.

### Charts

"Subscriber growth" shows new subscribers, unsubscribes and net growth, with the "Net for the period" total
above the chart. "Message volume" shows incoming and outgoing messages. If the period fits within one day, the
charts go by hour, otherwise by day. On the "All time" tab the charts show the last 90 days. If there were no
events, the chart says "No data for the selected period".

### Bots

The "Bots" table lists every bot of the company, how many of its subscribers haven't blocked it, and its
status. "Active" means the bot works. Otherwise the status gives the reason: "Token revoked", "Access
denied", "Delivery error" or "Unavailable". The bot's name takes you to the bot.

### Recent broadcasts

Up to eight of the company's latest broadcasts for the selected period. A row shows the start of the
broadcast text, the bot name and the time, and a broadcast without text reads "No text". On the right you
see how many subscribers got the broadcast out of all of them, as on the broadcast card, and the status:
"Scheduled", "Sending", "Completed", "Partial", "Failed" or "Cancelled". Drafts do not appear here, and a
cancelled broadcast shows only if someone got it before the cancel. The detailed statistics of a broadcast
open in [Broadcasts](/en/broadcasts).

### If you are not a company admin

If you don't have company admin rights, the Dashboard shows a "My bots" list instead of the summary: the bots
of every workspace you have access to. If you have access to none, it says "You don't have access to any bots
in this company yet."

## Workspace dashboard

The funnel of the bots of one workspace, under the "Workspace activity" subtitle. This helps when a company
has several teams or clients: each looks at its own dashboard and is not distracted by someone else's
numbers.

The period is switched with the tabs "Today", "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days", "All time" and "Custom", "Today"
opens by default. Under the header is the number of the workspace's bots and their active subscribers.

The top block shows "Active subscribers". If deposits with an amount came in during the period, "Deposit
volume" and the number of FTD show next to it. While there are no bots or they have no active subscribers,
this block is replaced by a "Get started" prompt with a "Go to workspaces" button. How to connect your first
bot is described in [Getting started](/en/getting-started).

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscribes** | How many people subscribed to your bots during the period. |
| **Contacts** | How many subscribers sent the bot their first message. |
| **Registrations** | Registrations from affiliate network postbacks. |
| **FTD** | First deposits from affiliate network postbacks. |
| **Redeposit** | Repeat deposits from affiliate network postbacks. |

Next to each number you see by how many percent it changed against the previous period of the same length.

The "Trends" chart shows subscribes, contacts, registrations, FTD and RD. If the period fits within one day,
the chart goes by hour, otherwise by day. On the "All time" tab it shows the last 90 days.

"Top campaigns" holds the five campaigns with the most FTD in the period, and with equal FTD the most
subscribes. Each shows its FTD and subscribes. "Top bots" holds the five bots with the most FTD, each with
its FTD and the number of subscribers who haven't blocked it. If there were no events in the period, "Top
campaigns" says "No data for the period".

A campaign in "Top campaigns" opens in the tracker, and the "All" link leads to the tracker's campaign list.
Only the campaigns available to you in the tracker open: for a buyer their own, for a team lead buyer their
own and their buyers', for the owner every campaign of the company, for an administrator with the "Tracker"
capability every campaign of the workspaces in their access scope. Other campaigns are listed in the top but
do not open. Without tracker access there is no "All" link.

## Bot dashboard

The "Dashboard" screen in the bot menu: the bot's name, its @username and the same funnel as the workspace
has, for one bot. It has the same period tabs, the top block, the metrics, "Trends" and this bot's "Top
campaigns". Campaigns in the top open in the tracker the same way as on the workspace Dashboard.

If Telegram doesn't let the platform work with the bot, "Bot unavailable" shows at the top with the reason.
While the bot has no active subscribers, the top block is replaced by a "Get started" prompt.

<Tip>
  "No data for the selected period" is not an error: there were no events on the selected dates. Widen the
  period.
</Tip>

## How this differs from the tracker dashboard

The Dashboard answers "how are things going", while the tracker dashboard answers "how is traffic buying
working". The tracker has its own funnel that starts at the ad click, its own cost metrics and its own
breakdowns by campaign, source and landing.
