The Dashboard is the first screen after you log in. It exists at three levels, and at each one it answers a
different question: how the whole company is doing, how a particular team is doing and how a particular bot
is doing.

## Company dashboard

An overview of the company's activity for the selected period. Metrics at the top, trends and top lists
below.

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Active subscribers** | How many subscribers are currently active across all bots of the company. |
| **Subscribes** | How many people started your bots during the period. |
| **Contacts** | How many subscribers sent their first message. |
| **Registrations** | Registrations that arrived as postbacks from affiliate networks. |
| **Redeposit** | Repeat deposits during the period. |
| **Deposit volume** | Total deposits during the period. |

The period is switched with the tabs "Today", "Yesterday", "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days", "All time" and
"Custom". The "Trends" chart shows subscribes, contacts and registrations by day of the selected period.

The "Top bots" and "Top campaigns" blocks show where the work is happening: bots show their subscriber
count, and the "All" button on campaigns leads to the tracker's campaign list.

<Info>
  If you have no bots yet, the metrics are replaced by a "Get started" prompt with a "Go to workspaces"
  button. How to connect your first bot is described in [Getting started](/en/getting-started).
</Info>

## Workspace dashboard

The same set of metrics, but limited to the bots of that workspace. This helps when a company has several
teams or clients: each looks at its own dashboard and is not distracted by someone else's numbers.

The "My bots" block lists the workspace bots you have access to. If you have access to none, the block says
so.

## Bot dashboard

The "Dashboard" section in the bot menu. It shows the metrics of a single bot and its recent broadcasts.

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | How many of the bot's subscribers are active. |
| **Blocked** | How many subscribers blocked the bot. |
| **New today** and **New this week** | Subscriber inflow. |
| **Subscribes**, **Contacts**, **Registrations**, **FTD**, **RD** | The bot's funnel for the period. |
| **Reg2FD** | The share of registrations that reached a first deposit. |
| **CR** | The share of subscribes that reached a first deposit. |

Below there is the "Daily trend" chart and two broadcast blocks: "Recent broadcasts" with their statuses
("Draft", "Scheduled", "Sending", "Completed", "Cancelled", "Failed") and "Scheduled broadcasts".

<Tip>
  If there are no metrics for the period, the block says "No data for this period". That is not an error: it
  means there were no events on those dates. Widen the period.
</Tip>

## How this differs from the tracker dashboard

The Dashboard answers "how are things going", while the tracker dashboard answers "how is traffic buying
working". The tracker has its own funnel that starts at the ad click, its own cost metrics and its own
breakdowns by campaign, source and landing.
