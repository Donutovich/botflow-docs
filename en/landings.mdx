A landing is a promo page between the ad click and the move into the bot. For Facebook traffic it is required:
the landing is what carries the pixel and separates the ad click from the move into Telegram. For direct Telegram
traffic no landing is needed, the link leads into the bot directly.

## Where landings live

In the company's left menu open the **Tracker** section and go to the **Landings** tab. Here you see every
landing of the company, its status and its metrics. The **New landing** button starts creating one.

<Info>
  Landings, like the whole tracker, are available to members with a tracker role ("Buyer", "Team lead buyer") and
  to company administrators. A member without a tracker role does not see the tab. For roles see
  [Settings and access](/en/settings).
</Info>

## Two landing types

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Telegram clone" icon="clone">
    A copy of the public `t.me/<bot>` page. The heading, avatar and description are taken from the bot's settings in @BotFather. Nothing to upload.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom HTML" icon="file-code">
    A ZIP archive with your own `index.html`. Any markup you like. A placeholder goes into the link of the transition button, where the platform substitutes the link to the bot. Up to 50 MB.
  </Card>
</Columns>

The type is chosen at creation and determines the set of settings. The landing's name (for example, "@mybot main
t.me" or "VIP funnel") is visible only to you and your team and never reaches the ads.

## Telegram clone

The clone repeats the look of the public `t.me/<bot>` page, but without the Telegram header and without an
automatic redirect: ad networks forbid such a redirect. The transition button leads into the bot with a link like
`t.me/<bot>?start=<click_id>`.

At creation you choose a bot, and the page fields are pulled from its information in @BotFather. Any field can be
overridden in the landing's settings. A field left empty takes the current value from the bot.

| Field | What it shows | If empty |
|---|---|---|
| **Heading** | The name above the button | The bot's current name from @BotFather |
| **About** | The description under the heading | The bot's short description (`/setabouttext`) |
| **Extra** | A line under the heading | The bot's `@username` |
| **Button text** | The label on the transition button | `Start Bot` |
| **Photo** | The page's picture | The bot's avatar |
| **Footer note** | The text under the button | The standard note about starting in Telegram |

The photo is uploaded as JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB. If you delete the photo, the bot's avatar is substituted
again.

<Note>
  Edits to a clone apply to the whole page at once: to the heading, the description and to the preview shown when
  the link is sent in messengers.
</Note>

## Custom HTML

If you need your own design, upload a ZIP archive with your landing. An `index.html` file is required inside the
archive (in the root or in a single nested folder). The maximum archive size is 50 MB.

For the transition button to lead into the bot, its link (href) must contain the `{{offer}}` placeholder. When the
page is shown, the platform replaces it with a personal `t.me/<bot>?start=<click_id>` link carrying that visitor's
click code.

```html
<a href="{{offer}}">Open in Telegram</a>
```

You can also use the `{{click_id}}` placeholder inside the HTML if you want to pass the click code to your own
scripts or pixels on the page.

<Warning>
  If `{{offer}}` is not found in the archive, the landing still uploads, but a warning appears next to it saying
  the `{{offer}}` placeholder was not found and the button will not be able to lead into the bot. Check that it
  sits exactly in the button's href and upload the archive again.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  The `{{offer}}` and `{{click_id}}` placeholders are written exactly like that, in double curly braces. Single
  braces (`{offer}`) are not recognised and not replaced.
</Tip>

Uploading a ZIP again replaces the previous version of the landing. The other files from the archive (styles,
images, scripts) are served together with the page, and nothing needs to be linked from elsewhere.

## The default landing

For every bot the platform automatically creates a Telegram clone marked **Default**. It always reflects the
bot's current settings from @BotFather, so it cannot be edited or deleted; edit the bot's profile instead. If you
need your own heading or picture, create a separate Telegram clone and configure it.

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Created but not ready to be shown (for example, a custom landing with no ZIP uploaded). |
| **Deploying** | The page is being prepared. |
| **Active** | Ready to be shown, can be assigned to campaigns. |
| **Failed** | Something went wrong during preparation, the landing cannot be shown. |

A Telegram clone becomes active straight after creation. A custom landing activates once you have uploaded a
correct ZIP with `index.html`.

## Landing metrics

Clicks, views and moves into the bot are counted per landing. A landing view (**LP view**) is recorded separately
from a click: a click is recorded when the page is requested, while an LP view is marked once the page actually
rendered for the visitor. That lets you see what share of clicks reached the landing and pressed the button into
the bot.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Clicks** | Visits to the landing through the tracking link. |
| **LP views** | Actual renders of the page for a visitor. |
| **Bot starts** | Moves into the bot from this landing (a subscriber is created). |
| **Click2LP** | The share of clicks that reached a landing view. |
| **LP2Bot** | The share of landing views that moved into the bot. |
| **Click2Bot** | End-to-end conversion from click to bot start. |

Metric names are the same in every interface language. The period is chosen at the top of the tab: a day, 7 days,
30 days, all time or a custom range. The list can be sorted by any numeric column, and the set of columns is
configured with the "Columns" button.

<Note>
  A landing click and a bot start are different events. A landing click is a hit on the promo page, and it may
  never reach the bot. Starting the bot with `/start` creates a subscriber and opens the funnel. In the tracker's
  reports they are separated. More in [Tracker: overview](/en/tracker).
</Note>

## Connecting a landing to traffic

A landing does not accept traffic on its own. It has to be assigned to a campaign: then visits through the
campaign's link open that landing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the landing" icon="window-maximize">
    Assemble a Telegram clone or upload a custom ZIP and bring it to the "Active" status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose it in the campaign" icon="link">
    In the campaign wizard, on the "Entry point" step, set the bot and the landing. For the Facebook source the landing carries the pixel. How to create a campaign is described in [Campaigns](/en/campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the traffic" icon="arrow-pointer">
    Hand out the campaign's tracking link. Every visitor sees the landing, and the button brings them into the bot tied to a `click_id`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The Facebook pixel is set in the campaign settings rather than in the landing: the platform inserts it into the
  page when it is shown. The PageView event fires on a landing view, and server events go through the Conversion
  API. For the pixel and postbacks see [Postbacks and conversions](/en/postbacks).
</Info>

## Managing the list

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Search and filter" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Search by name and a filter by status: "All", "Active", "Drafts".
  </Card>
  <Card title="Preview" icon="eye">
    The preview button opens the landing in a new tab. It is a service view: the pixel does not fire and the view does not land in the metrics.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Editing" icon="pen">
    Change a clone's fields or replace a custom landing's ZIP. The "Default" landing cannot be edited.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deleting" icon="trash">
    A landing can be deleted one at a time or in bulk by selecting several rows. The "Default" landing cannot be deleted.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  A buyer sees and edits their own landings, a team lead buyer sees their buyers' landings and their own, and a
  company administrator sees them all.
</Note>
