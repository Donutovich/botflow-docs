Besides bots, you can connect a regular Telegram account to a workspace. Private chats of this Telegram account
with people will appear in the "Messages" section: workspace members will be able to read and reply to them, as
in Telegram itself. The other person sees a real person, not a bot.

<Note>
  Only private chats with people come to the platform. Groups, channels, bots, "Saved Messages" and Telegram
  service messages are not shown in BotFlow. You can't start a new chat from BotFlow: the chats come from
  Telegram itself.
</Note>

## Read before connecting

Before the login BotFlow shows a screen with the terms of connecting. BotFlow will log in to your Telegram account
as one more device. BotFlow is not affiliated with Telegram: it is a third-party client that works through the
Telegram API.

The risks this screen warns about:

- Telegram may restrict or ban a Telegram account that works through third-party services. No service can
  guarantee that this won't happen: Telegram makes the decision.
- Telegram limits the pace of messages: when you send often, it pauses or limits sending.
- Telegram may show the people you chat with a mark in your profile saying that you use an unofficial Telegram
  client.
- A BotFlow session will appear in the Devices section in Telegram. Its login location there will be that of our
  server, not your city.
- You can disconnect the Telegram account in BotFlow or in Telegram itself: "Settings" → "Devices", the BotFlow
  session, "Terminate Session".

<Tip>
  Connect a work Telegram account that has been in use for a long time and is tied to a regular SIM card: Telegram
  restricts new and virtual numbers faster.
</Tip>

### What BotFlow does on behalf of the Telegram account

- Receives the messages of private chats and loads earlier ones when an employee scrolls a chat up.
- Sends the replies of employees.
- Shows the other person that the Telegram account is typing while an employee writes a reply.
- Deletes sent messages for both sides when an employee deletes them.
- Blocks and unblocks the other person when an employee does so.
- Marks messages as read when an employee replies.
- Downloads photos, videos and files when an employee opens them in a chat.
- Downloads the profile photos of the Telegram account and of the people you talk to when they show on screen.
- Never writes first to people the Telegram account has no chat with.

The bottom of the screen, above the "Continue" button, shows the price: "$1.00 a day per connected Telegram
account, messages at the usual rates." The same line stands under the subtitle of the "Telegram accounts" section.
More in [Payment](#payment).

## Connecting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press «Connect» and choose «Telegram account»" icon="plug">
    Open the workspace and go to "Bots". The "Connect" button opens a menu: "Bot" connects a bot using a token
    from @BotFather, "Telegram account" connects a regular Telegram account using a QR code or phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the terms" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A screen with the terms opens first: the risks, what BotFlow does on behalf of the Telegram account, and the
    price. Press "Continue".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in by QR code" icon="qrcode">
    Open Telegram on your phone, go to "Settings" → "Devices" → "Add Device" and point your phone at the screen.
    Scan the code with another device where Telegram is open: a phone can't scan its own screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or log in by phone number" icon="phone">
    Press "Log in by phone number", confirm your country code and enter the phone number of the Telegram account.
    Telegram will send a login code, most often to the Telegram app on your other device, sometimes by SMS, a
    phone call, email or through Fragment. Enter it in the "Code" field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the password" icon="key">
    If the Telegram account is protected with an additional password, enter it in the "Password" field. If you
    forgot it, recover it in the Telegram app on a device where you are already logged in: "Settings" → "Privacy
    and Security" → "Two-Step Verification".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done" icon="circle-check">
    You will see "Telegram account connected". The Telegram account's card appears in the "Telegram accounts"
    section of the "Bots" page, and its chats start appearing in the "Messages" section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Don't forward the code or take a screenshot of it: Telegram will cancel such a code. Copy it or type it in.
  Don't request the code many times in a row: Telegram limits the number of logins by phone number per day.
</Warning>

### Who can connect

The company owner or an administrator can connect a Telegram account to a workspace, and so can a manager of that
workspace. Only the person who started a login sees it.

Telegram accounts can be connected when the company has money on its balance, wherever it came from: a top-up, a
promo code, a bonus or a promo credit. A company with no money on its balance connects them after a top-up of $50 or
more.

Until then the "Telegram account" item of the "Connect" menu shows the line "You can connect Telegram accounts after
the first top-up of $50 or more." For those who can top up the balance, this item opens the "Top up the balance"
screen with the "Top up balance" button, which leads to [Billing](/en/billing). For everyone else the item says:
"The company owner or an admin with billing access can top up the balance." Billing access comes with the "Billing"
toggle described in [Administrator capabilities](/en/settings#administrator-capabilities).

While the company's balance is exhausted, a new Telegram account can't be connected.

### How many you can connect

The number of Telegram accounts is limited for the whole company, not per workspace. The subtitle of the "Telegram
accounts" section shows how many are connected and how many you can have, for example "Connected in the company: 1
of 2." When all places are taken, a new Telegram account can be connected once one of the connected ones is
disconnected or deleted. A Telegram account being deleted takes a place until BotFlow ends its session in Telegram.

A Telegram account connects to BotFlow in one place only. If it is already connected in another workspace or
another company, the login stops with "This Telegram account is already connected". Disconnect it where it is
connected, or terminate the BotFlow session in Telegram: "Settings" → "Devices".

### Login limits

- A login gets 10 minutes. If time runs out, start over.
- The code gets 5 attempts, and so does the password. When the attempts run out, start the login over: Telegram
  will send a new code.
- If the codes for this login have run out, log in by QR code or start the login over.
- One login at a time runs in a workspace. While another Telegram account is being connected there, BotFlow shows
  "Another Telegram account is being connected to this workspace right now" and in how many minutes to try again.
- The number of login attempts per hour and per day is limited, and so is the number of logins running at once in
  the company and across BotFlow. When a limit is reached, BotFlow shows "Too many attempts" and "Try again in …"
  with a countdown, and the "Start over" button becomes available when the time is up. If the wait is unknown or
  longer than a day, "Please try again later." shows instead of the countdown.
- The number of logins with one phone number per day is limited too. Then "Too many attempts. Please try again
  later." appears above the number field: enter another number or log in by QR code.

### The Telegram account won't connect

Check:

- whether Telegram is open on the device you scan the QR code with;
- whether the number and the country code are entered correctly;
- whether the 10 minutes for the login have run out;
- whether Telegram has restrictions on this Telegram account.

If BotFlow says "Logging in by number won't work", Telegram asks to confirm the login in a way BotFlow doesn't
have: log in by QR code. "The login was canceled in Telegram" means that "Terminate", "Undo" or "No, it's not
me!" was tapped on another device. Start the login over.

## Chats

The Telegram account's card in the "Bots" section opens its chats. In the bot switcher of the navigation bar
Telegram accounts have their own "Telegram accounts" group. The Telegram account menu has three groups: "Inbox"
with "Messages", "Audience" with "Tags" and "Setup" with "Flow builder", "Disclosure requests" and "Settings".

Everyone with access to the workspace can read the chats and reply. An anonymous operator sees the other people
anonymised, as with bots, and can request data disclosure. How roles work is described in
[Settings](/en/settings#telegram-accounts).

### First fill

Right after connecting, BotFlow walks through the Telegram account's chat list from the newest to the oldest, then
through the archive, and the chats appear in the "Messages" section as it goes. For a Telegram account with many
chats this takes a while. Messages that were in a chat before connecting are loaded from Telegram when you scroll
the chat up.

### Chat list

The chat list, the search, the dialog and the "Info" panel work as in the bots' [Messenger](/en/messenger). The
differences:

- **Filters.** There are three modes, as in Telegram: "All chats", "Unread" and "Archived Chats". Chats from the
  Telegram archive are not part of "All chats" or of the unread counter, but the search finds them.
- **The "unread" mark.** A chat marked as unread in Telegram carries a counter without a number. The mark is removed
  when a team member replies in that chat or when the chat is opened in Telegram.
- **The "Info" panel.** It holds the other person's tags, last activity, "Leading the dialog", "User blocked",
  contact, the Click ID under "Traffic source", the "Deposits" block, the "Block" button and the "Media" tab.
  "Leading the dialog" names the team member whose reply from BotFlow last went to the other person, and the time of
  that reply. The registration and the deposits in the "Deposits" block come from the affiliate's postbacks.
  Telegram accounts have no funnel or AI.

### What a chat shows

- The other person's profile photo in the chat list, at the top of the chat and in the "Info" panel. If the
  person has no photo or has hidden it, their initials stand in its place, as in Telegram. An anonymous operator
  doesn't see a photo set for the contact in the Telegram account itself: the initials stand in its place too.
- Incoming and outgoing messages, including the ones sent from the phone and from other Telegram apps.
- Edited messages are marked "edited". A message deleted in Telegram is replaced by a "Message deleted" plaque:
  its content is not kept.
- Calls show as a line in the chat: "Incoming Call", "Outgoing Call", "Missed Call", "Canceled Call", and video
  calls too. You can't call from BotFlow.
- Chat events show as a centred line: a pinned message, auto-delete turned on and off, "History was cleared", a
  contact joining Telegram, the other person's reaction ("Reaction"). A reaction changes neither the chat preview
  nor the unread state.
- Messages with auto-delete disappear from BotFlow too when their timer runs out.
- Self-destructing photos and videos open only in Telegram on the phone. In the chat a line stands in their place,
  for example "Self-destructing photo".
- Messages BotFlow does not show appear as the line "This message is currently not supported."
- What the other person is doing right now: "typing", "recording voice", "recording video", "sending a photo",
  "sending a video" or "sending file". It shows at the top of the chat and in the chat list in place of the last
  message, as in Telegram.

### Files

BotFlow downloads photos, videos, voice messages, video messages, audio and documents from Telegram when they are
opened. Opening a large file for the first time may take a while. Files over 100 MB don't open in BotFlow: you can
see them in Telegram. While a Telegram account is disconnected, the files of its chats don't open. If a file
didn't open, you see "Couldn't load" and the "Retry" button in its place.

### History and how long it is kept

BotFlow keeps the messages of Telegram accounts for the last 90 days. Older messages are removed from BotFlow only,
they stay in Telegram. The last message of every chat is always kept, so a chat nobody wrote in for a long time
does not drop out of the list.

Older messages are loaded from Telegram when you scroll the chat up. Loaded messages are kept for 90 days from the
moment of loading. If Telegram asks to wait, the top of the chat shows when loading resumes, for example "Loading
resumes at 14:30." If loading failed, you see "Couldn't load" and the "Retry" button. While the Telegram account
is disconnected, old messages are not loaded.

### Blocking the other person

The "Block" button in the "Info" panel, under "Actions", blocks the other person in Telegram itself, like blocking
in the Telegram app. Before blocking, BotFlow asks for confirmation, as Telegram does. The "Unblock" button lifts
the block at once, without asking. While Telegram carries out a block or an unblock, the button is unavailable. If
Telegram refused, BotFlow shows the reason, and the button can be pressed again.

While the other person is blocked, the message field is closed with the caption "User blocked", and [blocks on
postback events](#blocks-on-postback-events) don't fire for them: the chat shows just the event. Both start right
after pressing "Block" and end right after pressing "Unblock". The "User blocked" row in the "Info" panel shows when
Telegram blocked the other person. Blocking and unblocking in the Telegram app show in BotFlow the same way.

## Reads

A chat of a Telegram account stays unread until someone replies to the other person, as with bots. Opening the chat
in BotFlow and reading it on the phone do not clear unread. A reply does: a team member's message from BotFlow, a
reply from the phone or from other Telegram apps, and also the Telegram Business "Greeting Message" and "Away
Message". Calls and chat events do not change unread: a missed call does not make a chat unread, and an outgoing
call does not count as a reply. While a team member's message waits to be sent and shows the clock, the chat stays
in "Unread".

Once Telegram confirms that a reply from BotFlow was sent, BotFlow marks the other person's messages as read in
Telegram itself too: the other person sees two check marks together with the reply, and on the phone the chat
becomes read. Until there is a reply, BotFlow does not mark the chat as read in Telegram. Messages the other person
wrote after the reply stay unread.

On the first connection, BotFlow takes the unread state from Telegram: a chat read on the phone before connecting
comes in as read and becomes unread with the other person's next message.

On your messages one check mark means "Unread" and two check marks mean "Read". The same marks stand next to the
last message in the chat list.

<Note>
  Voice and video messages played in BotFlow stay unplayed in Telegram.
</Note>

## Replies and sending pace

### Replying

From a Telegram account's chat you send text and attachments: photos, videos, voice messages, video messages,
audio and albums of up to 10 photos and videos. The paperclip button attaches them by the same rules as with bots,
described in [Media](/en/messenger#media). To reply to a particular message, choose "Reply" in its menu.
[Reply templates](/en/messenger#reply-templates) work as with bots, templates with attachments included.

While a team member types a reply, the other person sees in Telegram that the Telegram account is typing.

A message sent from BotFlow carries the name of the team member who wrote it next to the time, as with bots: see
[Operator signature](/en/messenger#operator-signature).

While a message is being sent, a clock stands next to it. If sending failed, the message shows "Not sent" and the
reason. A message with attachments may go out to Telegram as several messages, for example an album and a voice
message. If the other person received only part of it, the reason is "The other person received only part of the
message" followed by why the rest did not go out.

If BotFlow refused the message or no answer to the send arrived, for example because the connection dropped, the
text goes back into the input. Sending again does not deliver a second copy if the first one went out. If another
chat is open by then or the input already holds a new text, an error notice appears with the message text and a
"Copy text" button. The notice stays on screen until you close it or copy the text.

### Deleting and cancelling

Your own message sent from BotFlow is deleted through the message menu, item "Delete": the message will be deleted
for you and for the other person. A colleague's message can be deleted by a member with the manager role or above.
Messages sent from the phone are deleted in Telegram itself. A deleted message leaves a plaque with the name of who
deleted it, for example "Message deleted by Ivan".

A message still waiting to be sent can be deleted, and it won't go out. If it is already being sent, BotFlow
answers "The message is already being sent. You can delete it once it is sent."

### Resending

A message that was not sent has a "Resend" item in its menu. The item is missing when a resend would change
nothing: the other person is unavailable or deleted their account, accepts messages only from Premium users or
charges for each message, the Telegram account itself blocked them, the text is too long, an attachment was
deleted or Telegram did not accept it, or the other person doesn't accept voice and video messages.

For a message the other person received only in part, "Resend" sends only what did not get there: the other
person won't see the part they have a second time.

### Sending pace

Telegram limits the pace of messages, so BotFlow sends the messages of one Telegram account no more often than
once every 5 seconds. If you press send earlier, a hint "Try again in 0:04." appears over the button and the text
stays in the field. When Telegram asks to wait, this interval grows, but to a minute at most, and then gradually
returns to 5 seconds.

### Chats where the other person hasn't written yet

In a chat where the other person has never written to the Telegram account, BotFlow sends more carefully:

- no more than one message every 3 minutes;
- no more than 20 messages to such chats in any 24 hours;
- in the first 72 hours after connecting, no more than 5 messages in any 24 hours.

A message over these limits waits its turn. Once the other person replies, the limit no longer applies to their
chat.

### How long a message waits

A message waits to be sent for 15 minutes at most. If it couldn't go out in that time, it becomes "Not sent" with
the reason "Not sent in time". If Telegram set a pause longer than the message can wait, the reason is "Telegram
limit, try resending".

### Sending paused

If Telegram asked the Telegram account to wait before sending, its state is "Sending paused", and the card shows
when sending resumes, for example "Sending resumes at 14:30." Messages that can wait until the end of the pause
go out by themselves.

### Restricted

If Telegram considered the sending to be spam, it restricts the Telegram account. Its state is "Restricted":
"Telegram has restricted sending messages. You can reply to those who message you first." Messages to chats where
the other person hasn't written yet become "Not sent" right away. If Telegram also forbids replies, messages go
out to no chat at all.

Some of these restrictions lift by themselves within a few hours. You can check the restriction with
[@SpamBot](https://t.me/SpamBot) inside Telegram. Once Telegram lifts the restriction, the owner or an
administrator presses "Limit lifted" on the Telegram account's card or on its settings page. If the restriction is
still in place, Telegram sets it again after the very first send, and the state comes back.

### Why a message was not sent

| Reason | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Not sent in time | The message did not go out within 15 minutes. |
| Telegram limit, try resending | Telegram's pause ends later than the message could wait. |
| Telegram has restricted sending messages | The Telegram account is restricted, and the other person hasn't written first. |
| Telegram only allows reading messages | The Telegram account is frozen. |
| Telegram has not confirmed sending | BotFlow doesn't know whether the message reached the other person. |
| The other person blocked the Telegram account | The other person no longer accepts messages from this Telegram account. |
| User blocked | The Telegram account itself blocked the other person. You can unblock them in the "Info" panel. |
| The other person is unavailable on Telegram | Telegram doesn't let you write to this person. |
| Telegram account disconnected | The Telegram account was disconnected or stopped before sending. |
| The other person received only part of the message | The beginning of the message is with the other person already. Why the rest did not go out follows. |
| The media file is unavailable because it was deleted | The attachment was deleted from BotFlow. Upload it again and resend. |
| Telegram did not accept the file | Telegram refused the attachment. |
| The other person's name and "doesn't accept voice and video messages" | The other person forbade sending them voice and video messages. |

## Tracking clients

The other person in a Telegram account's chat gets a Click ID as soon as they appear in BotFlow, the same as a bot
subscriber. The Click ID is shown in the "Info" panel under "Traffic source", with a copy button next to it.

### A link with the Click ID

For the partner network to tie a registration and a deposit to the other person, send them a link with the
`{{ click_id }}` variable in a message or a [reply template](/en/messenger#reply-templates). On sending, BotFlow puts
that person's Click ID into the link. The partner network's postbacks find the other person by the Click ID the same
way as a bot subscriber: see [Postbacks](/en/postbacks).

### Blocks on postback events

The Telegram account menu has a "Flow builder" section. Telegram account blocks fire on postback events:
"Registration", "Deposit" and "Repeat deposit". The event is picked in the block condition from the "System events"
list; clicking it again removes it from the condition. A block without an event does not fire.

The block's "Actions" offer:

- "Add tag" and "Remove tag": the tag is applied to the conversation with the other person and is managed in the
  "Tags" section.
- "Send API request" to an "External URL" or to "Another bot", as in the bots'
  [Flow builder](/en/constructor#send-api-request). Variables work in the request address, for example
  `{{ click_id }}` and `{{ event.payout }}`.

The Telegram account sends no messages, buttons or follow-ups from blocks, and it has no arrows between blocks.

Blocks keep firing when the Telegram account is disconnected: after the "Disconnect" button in BotFlow and after its
session ends, for example if Telegram ended it or it was ended in Telegram itself. A [bot that is
off](/en/bot-settings#activity) works the same way. Blocks don't fire only for a deleted Telegram account and for
the other person the Telegram account blocked: then the chat shows just the event. As with bots, blocks don't fire while the company's balance is exhausted.

### Registrations and deposits in campaigns

Registrations and deposits of a Telegram account's contacts show in [Campaigns](/en/campaigns), in the row of this
Telegram account with the "Organic" source. The row appears with the first postback, even when it comes after the
Telegram account was deleted. The owner and the administrators of the company see it.

## Telegram account states

The state shows on the Telegram account's card in the "Bots" section and on its settings page, with the reason in
a line below it.

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Connected | The Telegram account works: messages come in and go out. |
| Sending paused | Telegram asked to wait before sending. The card shows when sending resumes. |
| Restricted | Telegram restricted sending: you can reply to those who message you first. |
| Frozen | Telegram only allows reading messages. The "Submit an appeal" button leads to the appeal in Telegram: appeal from this Telegram account before the date on the card, or Telegram will delete it. |
| Stopped | The company's work is stopped, for example the balance is exhausted. Messages don't come in or go out until the company works again. |
| Disconnecting | BotFlow is ending its session in Telegram. |
| Disconnected | The BotFlow session in Telegram has ended. The chats stay in BotFlow, the reason is on the card. |
| Banned | Telegram banned this Telegram account. |

The disconnect reasons on the card:

- "The session was terminated in Telegram." The BotFlow session was ended in Telegram itself, in the Devices
  section.
- "Disconnected by an employee." The owner or an administrator pressed "Disconnect".
- "Disconnected for non-payment." The company's balance stayed exhausted for 30 days.
- "Disconnected by the platform." The BotFlow platform ended the session.
- "The session broke off because of a BotFlow failure."
- "The Telegram account was deleted in Telegram." Such a Telegram account can't be connected again.

## Telegram account settings

The "Settings" item of the Telegram account menu, or "Telegram account settings" in its card's menu, opens a page
with information about the Telegram account and actions:

- **Profile photo, "Name in Telegram" and username.** They are changed in the Telegram app.
- **Phone number.** Only the owner and the company administrators see it.
- **"Connected on".**
- **"Title".** The Telegram account's name for your team, up to 64 characters. Only company staff see it, people in
  Telegram don't. When a title is set, BotFlow shows the Telegram account under it. The title is changed by the
  owner, administrators and managers of the workspace.
- **"Service notifications".** The last 20 notifications Telegram sent to this Telegram account. The digits in
  them are hidden. Only the owner and the company administrators see them.
- **"Disconnect Telegram account" and "Delete Telegram account".** For the owner and administrators.

## Disconnecting and connecting again

The owner and administrators can disconnect a Telegram account: the "Disconnect" item of the card's menu or of the
settings page. BotFlow will end its session in the Telegram account. The chats will stay on the platform, and new
messages will stop coming. While a Telegram account is disconnected, including for non-payment, the files of its
chats don't open and the profile photos of the Telegram account and of the people show as initials. No rent is
charged for a disconnected Telegram account. You can also disconnect it in Telegram itself: "Settings" →
"Devices", the BotFlow session, "Terminate Session".

A disconnected Telegram account is connected again with the "Connect again" button on its card: log in to the same
Telegram account by QR code or phone number. You will see "Telegram account connected again" and "Your earlier
chats are kept."

Who can connect it again:

- after "Disconnected by an employee": the owner or an administrator;
- after "Disconnected for non-payment": the company owner or an administrator;
- in the other cases: whoever can connect Telegram accounts to this workspace;
- a Telegram account that was already in the company and is now disconnected in another workspace is brought back
  with its chats only by the company owner or an administrator.

## Deleting

The owner and administrators can delete a Telegram account: the "Delete" item of the card's menu or the "Delete
Telegram account" button on the settings page. The Telegram account will disappear from all lists, and BotFlow will
end its session in Telegram right away. Chats and tags will be deleted and cannot be restored. In Telegram itself
the chats stay.

While BotFlow is ending the session, the section shows the "Telegram account is being deleted" line, and the
Telegram account takes a place in the company's limit. The data is erased gradually.

What is erased: the chats and messages, the people the Telegram account talks to with their tags and variables,
the disclosure requests about them, the title for your team, and the files and profile photos of the Telegram
account and of the people BotFlow downloaded from Telegram.

What stays:

- the row of the Telegram account in "Campaigns" with its registrations and deposits. As with a deleted bot, it is
  kept for 365 days and then deleted. While it is kept, new postbacks about former contacts count in it, and blocks
  on events don't fire for them;
- the Click IDs of former contacts, without names or chats, so their postbacks still count. Each Click ID is kept
  for 365 days after the contact is erased and then deleted;
- log rows with the title or the name of the Telegram account, for example "Telegram account connected" and
  "Telegram account deleted". Log rows are kept for 180 days;
- log rows about disclosure requests together with the reason text of the request;
- the transaction history with the charges for the Telegram account.

The same Telegram account can be connected again, but as a new one: the chats come again from Telegram, while the
other people's tags and other data from BotFlow don't come back. The contacts get new Click IDs, and postbacks for
links sent before the delete count in the row of the deleted Telegram account. While its data is being erased and
for 30 days after that, only the owner or an administrator can connect it to this company.

<Note>
  To delete a workspace, delete its Telegram accounts first.
</Note>

## Payment

- **Rent.** $1 a day per connected Telegram account, including one with sending paused, a restricted one and a
  frozen one. A disconnected or deleted Telegram account is not paid for, and neither is any Telegram account
  while the company is stopped.
- **Messages.** Paid as with bots: incoming ones and the ones sent from BotFlow. Messages that were not sent are
  not paid for.
- **Free.** Messages sent from the phone, loaded history, calls and chat events.

When the balance is exhausted, the Telegram account's connection holds for 15 more minutes, so a payment a minute
late doesn't interrupt anything. Then the Telegram account is "Stopped": messages don't come in or go out. After a
top-up it starts receiving messages again, and incoming messages that arrived while the company was stopped are
not charged. After 30 days without payment BotFlow ends its session in the Telegram account: the card shows
"Disconnected for non-payment", and the company owner or an administrator can connect it again. More in
[Billing](/en/billing#telegram-accounts).

## Viewing by BotFlow staff

A BotFlow staff member can open the chats of a Telegram account only to view them: they can't reply, delete
messages or mark them as read. Every such opening is written to the logs as the row "Viewed by a platform admin".
A BotFlow staff member can change the constructor blocks and tags of a Telegram account, and such changes are
written as the row "Changed by a platform admin". The owner and the company administrators see both rows. More in
[Logs](/en/logs).
